x264 cannot code a 720x486 4:2:0 picture as interlaced

Task #92, 2026-11-21. Measured on an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 Pro) with the ffmpeg of deps 1.18.0-rc2 (FFmpeg 9.0.2, martin-riedl build, data/ffmpeg_version.txt). Found by task #81 (its report). Every number below comes from scripts/reproduce.sh; the raw output is in data/. No screenshots: nothing here is a visual claim.

Outcome

  • The fault: a job with Deinterlace off, a detected field order, H.264 (libx264, the default codec) and a 4:2:0 output 486 lines high (NTSC D1) failed at the encoder, before writing anything: [libx264] height not divisible by 4 (720x486). Since task #12 x264 codes a still-interlaced picture as fields (MBAFF: -flags +ildct -x264-params tff=1|bff=1), and libx264 refuses interlaced 4:2:0 unless each field's chroma is whole, i.e. the height is a multiple of 4.
  • H.264 itself cannot store it. In an interlaced stream (frame_mbs_only_flag=0) the crop unit for 4:2:0 is 2 x 4 rows (CropUnitY = SubHeightC × (2 − frame_mbs_only_flag)), so padding to 488 and declaring a 2-row crop is refused (§2). An interlaced 4:2:0 H.264 stream is never 486 lines high.
  • Decision: x264 codes such a picture progressive, unflagged, as x265 already is, and the job log says how to keep the fields: crop 2 lines with Crop & Resize, choose Color Format 4:2:2, or choose ProRes, v210 or FFV1. One decision, VideoJob::field_order_signal (with VideoJob::h264_cannot_code_fields_at), read by pipeline_executor::field_order_args and field_order_note. The note of the codecs that can't flag at all (x265, AV1, the hardware encoders) no longer suggests H.264 at such a size.
  • Only x264 is affected (§4): ProRes, v210, FFV1, HuffYUV, FFVHuff, x265, SVT-AV1 and VideoToolbox all encode 720x486.

Decisions

  • Progressive, not cropped or padded to fit. The frame size is the user's (Crop & Resize is the only thing that changes it), and a 2-row crop or pad would also shift the display aspect by 0.4 %. The user gets the interlaced option in one step: Crop & Resize already crops a still-interlaced 4:2:0 picture in steps of 4 lines.
  • Not refused. H.264 is the default codec and 720x486 an ordinary capture size; elsewhere a field-order shortcoming is said in the job log, never refused.
  • No container-only flag. -top on a progressive x264 stream makes MKV say tb and MOV say progressive: the container and the stream disagreeing is why x265 isn't flagged either.
  • Size unknown: as before. With no input size the worker keeps MBAFF.

Not tested

  • NVENC, Quick Sync and AMF (no hardware); x86 builds of libx264 (the height check is libx264's own and architecture-independent).
  • How players display the progressive-coded result; it carries no field flag, so they show it as progressive, combing on motion, exactly as an x265 encode of the same picture.
