DFTTest's patient FFTW plans differ between processes: plan with FFTW_ESTIMATE

2026-11-22 · task #101 (found by task #98) · measured on an Apple M1 (arm64, and the macOS x64 bundle under Rosetta) and on GitHub's hosted runners (Linux x64, Windows x64, macOS x64 and arm64), with the published deps 1.18.0-rc3 bundle (DFTTest with FFTW_PATIENT) and this task's 1.18.0-rc4 build (FFTW_ESTIMATE) · pictures: task #98's grain on flat grey and Tests/TestResources/interlaced_test.avi, both 720x480 4:2:0 at 8 and 16 bits · every number below is in data/, produced by scripts/.

Outcome: fixed, in deps 1.18.0-rc4. DFTTest plans its four FFTs with FFTW_PATIENT, which makes FFTW time candidate algorithms in the running process and keep the fastest. Different algorithms round floats differently, so two processes could filter the same frame differently, and a preview and its encode are two processes. On every x86 machine measured (Linux, Windows, and the macOS x64 bundle under Rosetta) DFTTest's 3D plans (tbsize > 1) gave two or three pictures across 20–30 fresh processes: the Noise Reduction DFTTest method, QTGMC's dfttest denoiser (and its knlmeanscl fallback) and TemporalDegrain2's postFFT 3. Its 2D plans (tbsize=1: havsfunc's SMDegrain and MCTemporalDenoise prefilters) never did. Apple Silicon agreed with itself every time. The pictures differ by 1 level (2 for QTGMC and TD2) in at most 0.135% of samples.

DFTTest has no argument that picks the planner, so Scripts/patches/dfttest-bc5e018-fftw-estimate.patch, applied after task #98's thread-safety patch on every platform, plans with FFTW_ESTIMATE: one plan per machine. With it, every key gave one picture on every machine, and six previews of one frame equal the lossless encode sample for sample (heavy test). It costs no measurable speed. Against PATIENT's pictures it moves only the 3D cases, by at most 2 levels in at most 0.13% of samples (on some x86 runners it is exactly one of PATIENT's pictures); the 2D plans, SMDegrain, DCTFilter and FFT3DFilter are unchanged.

FFTW's wisdom shows no problem shared between DFTTest and FFT3DFilter, so a DFTTest PATIENT plan could not have undone FFT3DFilter's ESTIMATE fix (task #80) in a mixed graph; FFT3DFilter's picture after DFTTest planned in the same process equals a fresh one's.

1. The question

Task #98 rebuilt DFTTest from source and checked its output against the old build's: under Rosetta both builds gave two pictures for DFTTest(tbsize=3) across ten fresh vspipe processes (5+5 and 1+9), the same two pictures. dfttestCreate (DFTTest.cpp, bc5e018) plans fftwf_plan_dft_r2c_3d/c2r_3d (tbsize × sbsize × sbsize) or the 2D pair with FFTW_PATIENT | FFTW_DESTROY_INPUT, one transform per plan, single-threaded. yuygfgg's fork (e6f580a, macOS arm64) has the same four lines. This is task #80's FFT3DFilter fault with a more patient planner. The questions: how often and by how much, on which platforms; whether a DFTTest problem is ever one of FFT3DFilter's (FFTW reuses a more patient plan for a later ESTIMATE request of the same problem, task #80); and what ESTIMATE costs.

2. Method

  • common.py holds the pictures and the calls (CASES): the Noise Reduction method at its defaults (nr_default: sigma 8, tbsize 3, sbsize 16, which are also DFTTest's own defaults, so this is task #98's case) and stronger (nr_strong: sigma 16, tbsize 5); tb3_sb12; 2D plans tb1 (havsfunc's prefilter call) and tb1_sb12; havsfunc's QTGMC with Denoiser='dfttest'; SMDegrain prefilter=3; the template's TemporalDegrain2 with postFFT=3. DCTFilter (PATIENT, kept by task #96's decision) and two FFT3DFilter calls are measured for information. 12 frames, 8 threads; QTGMC, SMDegrain and TD2 at 8 bits only, QTGMC on the interlaced clip only: 37 keys.
  • xproc.py hashes every key in one process; probe.py runs it in 20 (CI) or 30 (local) fresh processes, counts pictures per key, then writes out the two most common pictures of each split key and measures the largest difference and the share of samples that differ.
  • wisdom.py / wisdom_probe.py plan every DFTTest call (and, separately, every FFT3DFilter call) at 720x480 and 96x64, then read the process's wisdom from the bundle's own libfftw3f (fftwf_export_wisdom_to_string) and compare FFTW's problem hashes; and render FFT3DFilter after DFTTest in one process against fresh processes.
  • compare.py: the ESTIMATE build's picture of every key against each PATIENT picture seen in five fresh processes. speed.py / speed_runs.sh: creation time, time to first frame and fps over 120 frames at 720x480 and 1920x1080, the two builds alternating, three runs each.
  • Local: the published rc3 macOS bundles, and copies of them with DFTTest rebuilt by build_dfttest.sh, which runs download-deps-macos.sh's own build function with both patches (x86_64 through a -arch x86_64 shim). CI: a temporary workflow (.github/workflows/probe-dfttest-fftw.yml, removed before merge) installed rc3 and this branch's build-deps-* rc4 artifacts on each runner and ran the same scripts (fetch_ci.sh copied its results into data/ci/).

4. Preview = encode

app/test/integration_dfttest_determinism_test.dart (heavy) runs, through the worker, a lossless (FFV1) encode of frames 40–59 of colorize_test.mp4 and six previews each of frames 48 and 52: the Noise Reduction DFTTest method, QTGMC with Denoiser='dfttest' (single rate) and TemporalDegrain2 with postFFT=3, whose spatial pass is FFT3DFilter (the mixed graph). On the M1 with the ESTIMATE build all six previews and the encode agree, max difference 0, for every case, and so do they on CI's Linux x64 runners (both tiers) against this branch's rc4 bundle (nightly run 45001595186), where PATIENT gave more than one picture. The push gate checks the plugin's marker and that a second process hashes four DFTTest calls at two depths exactly as the first; it passed on all four platforms against the rc4 build (ci-test run 45001593065).

5. Limits

  • Rosetta translates x86 code; its plan timings are not an Intel CPU's. Its result matched the real x86 runners (the same 15 keys), and so did the Intel Mac runner.
  • A cross-process count is a sample: 20–30 processes can miss a rare plan, and a runner draw (CPU model, load) changes which plans win. Two CI runs per platform; every x86 job split the same keys.
  • ESTIMATE is one plan per machine, not per platform: two different CPUs may still pick different plans. A preview and its encode share a machine, so that is enough here.
  • The level differences are measured on two 720x480 pictures; other sizes and content were not swept.
  • The push-gate two-process check is probabilistic on a PATIENT build (both processes can pick the same plan); the plugin-name marker is the hard gate.
