Task #88: the script's YUV ↔ RGB on bt2020c, chroma-derived-* and ictcp

2026-11-21 · macOS arm64 (M1 Pro) · VapourSynth R78, deps 1.18.0-rc2 · branch stuart/88-script-side-yuv-rgb-borders-rife-ai-upsc

Summary

Five parts of the script convert between YUV and RGB through zimg: the border fill (every job with Add Borders or Pad to Fill, whatever the colour), RIFE, AI upscaling, Face Restoration and Colourisation. Each passed only the matrix (ColorMetadata::zimg_matrix). zimg refuses four matrices that way: 2020cl, chromancl, chromacl and ictcp ("Resize error 3074: invalid colorspace definition … May need to specify additional colorspace parameters"). So a source tagged bt2020c, chroma-derived-nc, chroma-derived-c or ictcp failed its whole job, encode and preview, as soon as one of those was on. Found by task #76.

Fixed: ColorMetadata::zimg_colorspace names the whole colour space (ZimgColorspace), and every conversion takes its arguments from it. Per tag:

Tagzimg readingWhy
bt709, fcc, bt470bg, smpte170m, smpte240m, bt2020nc, ycgco; rgb, smpte2085, untaggedunchanged (matrix only; the height guess)already converted; scripts byte-identical
bt2020c2020nclzimg's CL does not keep grey (§3) and refuses PQ/HLG (§1); same Kr/Kb; the preview's reading (#73)

The transfer and primaries go on both sides of every call (§1). Every conversion round-trips within 1 code (§2), so RIFE's real frames, Face Restoration outside its mask and Colourisation's luma stay bit-exact. Black and white bars are the same under every reading. This is the mixed approach, chosen over "the stand-in for every tag" (which would keep the preview matching the file, at the cost of passes seeing ICtCp pictures 50 levels off on average).

Every number below comes from scripts/probe_zimg.py, run by scripts/run.sh (the bundle's own Python). Raw output: data/probe-macos-arm64.txt.

Each matrix × transfer {none, 709, 601, 470bg, 2020_10, st2084, std-b67, srgb, linear} × primaries {none, 709, 170m, 470bg, 2020, st432-1}: an RGB24 swatch to 10-bit YUV, and 10-bit YUV to RGBS.

The transfer and primaries must be named on both sides. With them on the output only, even 709 fails RGB → YUV: the RGB swatch is untagged, and R78's resize copies an unset output colour space from the input, not the reverse. ZimgColorspace::to_yuv_args / from_yuv_args and ai_runtime.zimg_args always give both.

3. zimg's constant luminance does not keep grey

BT.2020's CL computes Yc from scene-linear RGB and encodes it with the OETF, so for R = G = B, Y′ = R′: grey 128 is 504 at 10-bit limited. zimg gives 434, and 16 gives 69 instead of 119. 434.6 is (128/255)2.4 put through the BT.709 OETF: zimg linearises R′G′B′ with the display EOTF (BT.1886) and encodes Y′c with the OETF. Its own round trip is consistent, but a decoder that follows the spec shows zimg's grey-128 bar at about 108. NCL keeps every neutral exact for any decoder, CL included (Cb = Cr = 0 decodes to R′ = G′ = B′ = Y′ either way). This, and the PQ/HLG refusal, is why bt2020c and chroma-derived-c are read as NCL rather than converted "exactly".

300 random RGB colours, each converted by zimg and by the standard's closed form (in the script), 10-bit limited codes:

chromancl, SMPTE 170M primariesmax 0max 89, mean 31 (BT.601)

ICtCp, HLG (BT.2100, scene-referred)max 84, mean 22.5max 377, mean 110 (2020ncl)

zimg's HLG ICtCp is not BT.2100's: it is display-referred (an OOTF on the way to linear), where BT.2100 defines ICtCp on scene light. Neutrals are kept and it round-trips, and it is about five times closer to the standard than the stand-in, so it is used. An ICtCp HLG file is vanishingly rare.

5. Primaries names

VapourSynth's resize names every primaries value FFmpeg reports except smpte428 (zimg: "unrecognized primaries"; xyz too) and jedec-p22 / ebu3213 (reachable only by number 22). A chroma-derived source with one of those is read as untagged.

Tests

  • worker/tests/zimg_colorspace_test.rs (push gate): every recognised matrix × primaries × transfer (and untagged) at 480 and 1080 lines, reduced to the 16 distinct colour spaces, run through the bundle's vspipe with the argument text the template receives: swatches at 4:2:0/4:2:2/4:4:4, 8–16 bits; round trip within 1 code (a quarter of an 8-bit level at 16 bits, where float32 through PQ moves up to 34 codes); black, white and (NCL) grey equal to the BT.709 reading; ai_runtime's helpers and Colourisation's float path. With bt2020c mapped back to 2020cl it fails with error 3074.
  • test_264 (script generation): each site's arguments, and byte-identical text for an ordinary tag.
  • app/test/integration_zimg_colour_tags_test.dart (heavy): Add Borders on a 10-bit 4:2:0 fixture per tag, black and a custom colour, encode and preview. NCL readings equal a twin tagged with the matrix, bit for bit; exact readings match the H.273 / BT.2100 closed forms within 1 code, the picture inside untouched. Colourisation keeps luma bit-exact and Face Restoration (no faces) every sample on bt2020c and ictcp PQ. RIFE (real frames bit-exact) and AI upscale run on them (gpu).

Limits

  • One platform measured; zimg is the same source in every bundle, and the push-gate test runs on all four.
  • Synthetic content (random colours, testsrc2).
  • The preview still misreads these tags where #73's swscale reading is the stand-in (chroma-derived with other primaries, ictcp): a custom bar colour or a pass's output shows in the preview as the source picture does. The encode is right.
  • ICtCp HLG is zimg's display-referred form (§4).
