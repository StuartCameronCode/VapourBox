MCTemporalDenoise's Sigma, and what replaced it

Task #141 (found by task #140, issue #177) · 2026-11-28 · measured on Apple Silicon (macOS arm64), deps 1.18.0-rc5, the debug worker

Summary

  • Sigma did nothing. The Noise Reduction pass's MCTemporalDenoise method passed its Sigma slider as sigma= to havsfunc r31's MCTemporalDenoise, which reads it only for its pfMode=0 (FFT3DFilter) prefilter. VapourBox never passes pfMode (havsfunc's default is 3, a DFTTest prefilter with a fixed sigma), so every Sigma gave the same picture, to the sample. The control and its field (mcTemporalSigma) are removed; saved jobs and presets that carry it still load.
  • Post-denoise replaces it (mcTemporalPost, advanced mode, default 0 = off): havsfunc's own post, an FFT3DFilter pass after the temporal denoise at that sigma. It acts strongly and in order (medium at noise σ 6: 34.5 dB → 43.1 at post 8), but it is spatial, so on a clean picture it costs 1.3–10.7 dB (up to 4.4 at σ 2), and it slows the pass by 1.3–1.8x. That is why it is off by default, and why the Strength steps don't use it. Off, the call is today's minus sigma=, which changes nothing.
  • pfMode=0 was the other way to make Sigma act, and was rejected: it replaces the prefilter for every job, so the default output would change, and it recovers less than post at heavy noise.
  • The low and medium profiles doing nothing on heavy noise is not SMDegrain's scene-change fault. Raising thSCD1 (issue #177's fix for SMDegrain) gains at most 0.7 dB; thSAD 600/1200 at most 0.15; limit=0 (no cap at the prefilter's change) about 3 dB at σ 6 (medium only) and nothing at σ 9, at 2–5 dB on clean pictures. Low and medium are profiles for light noise; high and very high (Strong and Very strong) are the ones for heavy noise, as the Strength steps' descriptions say. Nothing changes for it.
  • Found on the way: the preview of MCTD's high and very high profiles was not the encode. They run MDegrain twice and a stabilizing TTempSmooth, so they read up to 6 frames each side (8 with post at very high); the preview decoded 3, and high's preview was up to 2 levels off its lossless encode. The worker now declares MCTD's reach from its profile, radius and post (NoiseReductionParameters::mctd_reach), and the preview matches the encode sample for sample (integration_fft3dfilter_determinism_test.dart).

Method

Fixtures are issue #177's (report): 60 frames each of cam (small_clip.mp4, 320x240), film (colorize_test.mp4, 640x480) and dvbt (a broadcast field, 720x288), all 4:2:0 8-bit at 25 fps, lossless, and copies with the worker's AddGrain noise at luma σ 2, 4, 6 and 9 (chroma σ/2). Every MCTD run is an encode by the worker with the Noise Reduction pass off and the call in Custom VapourSynth, so it is the template's call plus the probed arguments, at radius 2 (the app's default), to lossless FFV1. The score is ffmpeg's luma PSNR against the clean clip, frames 0–2 skipped. Scripts: scripts/probe_grid.py (everything but the cut), scripts/probe_cut.py, scripts/tables.py and scripts/make_report.py (this page); data in data/.

SCRATCH=/your/scratch python3 ../issue-177-strength-mapping/scripts/build_fixtures.py
SCRATCH=/your/scratch JOBS=6 python3 scripts/probe_grid.py
SCRATCH=/your/scratch python3 scripts/probe_cut.py
python3 scripts/make_report.py

Limits. Synthetic, uncorrelated noise; three 60-frame clips; one machine (Apple Silicon); PSNR only (no visual review), which favours smoothing. Times are whole worker jobs run six at a time, including start-up, so only their ratios mean anything.

8. The preview's window

From havsfunc r31's defaults per profile (very low, low, medium, high, very high): twopass F F F T T, stabilize F F F T T, maxr 1 1 2 2 2, FFT3D bt 1 3 3 3 4. MDegrain reads radius frames each side, a second pass reads the first's neighbours (2 × radius), TTempSmooth maxr more, and post FFT3D's window (bt 2–3 one frame back, bt 4 two). So the reach is 2 (very low, radius 2) to 8 (very high, radius 2, with post), and 10 at radius 4. With the old fixed window of 3, two previews of frame 48 of colorize_test.mp4 at high (trim 40–59) agreed with each other and were up to 2 levels from the lossless encode (mean 0.0017); medium was exact. With the reach declared, high and very high, with and without post, are exact.
