One source model, owned by the worker

Task #50 (task #42's plan, R3a and R5). 2026-11-16. macOS arm64 (M-series), deps 1.16.0, debug worker.

What was built

vapourbox-worker --probe-source <path> prints a SourceModel: size, picture rate, frame count, video start offset, decoder delay, seek plan, grid, the stored frame times of a variable-rate source, colour tags, Dolby Vision, audio streams and the timestamp-reset scan. The app stores it on the queue item at analysis and sends it, unchanged, in every job (inline, or --source <file> for previews). The worker probes only when a job carries none. The app's own probe rules for the same facts (video start, decoder delay, seek plan, grid, frame count, rate choice, VFR test, SAR, colour tags) are deleted; its remaining twins are listed with their dispositions in docs/TWINS.md.

Not built: the app's Original picture and thumbnails through the worker (R3b, stage C of the plan). It waits for a go-ahead and its own latency measurement.

1. Nothing a decoder does changed

scripts/compare_preview.py runs the worker before the change and after it on every committed fixture (30 files) at frames 0, 1, 7, 40 and the third from last: the preview's decode-plan line (seek, trim, grid, delay) and the PNG it writes. All 146 cases identical, plan line and PNG bytes.

Limits: no variable-frame-rate fixture is committed (the heavy VFR suite, which generates them, passed); every pass off, so this tests the decode, not the passes; one platform.

2. The model says what the app used to compute

scripts/app_count_parity_test.dart ran the app's Dart probe rules (before they were deleted) next to --probe-source on the 30 fixtures: frame count, video start offset, decoder delay, seek-plan preroll, grid and picture rate. No difference on any fixture. The port is therefore a move, not a change, for every job the app sends.

The app's previews (every scrub) are 89–182 ms faster (about a third): the worker no longer probes on each one. On a variable-frame-rate source the old preview also read every packet of the file; the model carries those times. The new probe adds the reset scan to a job without a model (the DVB-T row's "probing" column).

Limits: small fixtures on an internal SSD. The two whole-file reads (the reset scan of a TS/PS and a VFR source's timestamps) already ran at analysis or on every preview; a 2-hour DVD or a NAS file was not timed.

Decisions

  • The app sends the model back unchanged (SourceModel.toJson is the worker's JSON), so a field the app doesn't read still reaches the worker and the Dart side derives nothing.
  • Values a job carries win over the model's, as the old fill_* probes never overwrote one; a model that no longer describes the file (path, size, modification time or schema) is re-probed.
  • idet and the soft-telecine flags stay in the app: they look at pictures, are not twins, and moving them deletes nothing.
  • The frame count of a job without one stays -count_frames (section 3).
