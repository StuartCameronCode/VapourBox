A delayed AVI cannot seek its sound near its start

2026-11-14 · macOS arm64, deps 1.16.0 (FFmpeg 9.0) · follow-up to issue #173 (AVI seeks)

Outcome: fixed. A trim of an AVI whose video has a decoder delay (H.264 with B-frames, MPEG-4 ASP, MPEG-2) from an in-point under ~0.12 s lost about 1.3 s of sound. #173 fixed the picture there by decoding from the start and counting frames; every decode of the sound still put an input -ss on the same file and hit the same failed seek. A trim from frame 2 of avi_h264_bframes.avi to MKV came out with its sound starting 1.277 s after the picture.

One decision, source_decode::sound_seek (rule 9): a delayed source's sound below COUNT_SEEK_BELOW (0.5 s, the picture's threshold) is never seeked with an input -ss.

  • Audio Restoration's render decodes from 0 and drops the head with atrim=start_sample + asetpts right after aresample=first_pts=0 (sample-exact, table 2).
  • Its analysis and transcription move the -ss after -i (exact).
  • The stream-copy encode (which never filters audio) seeks a NUT copy of the source's sound instead of the AVI. NUT keeps each stream's own time base, so its seek is sample-exact for PCM and MP3. MKA was 9–13 samples off (millisecond timestamps), an audio-only AVI 5 ms off on MP3.

The cause

fftools/ffmpeg_demux.c starts an input seek 3*AV_TIME_BASE/23 (0.13 s) early whenever any stream of the file has a decoder delay and the format lacks AVFMT_SEEK_TO_PTS. An AVI cannot seek before its start, so a target below that fails ("could not seek to position") and decoding continues about 1.4 s in. Mapping only the sound (-map 0:a, -vn) does not help: the check looks at every stream. MOV and MP4 copies of the same streams seek exactly.

Separate fault, not fixed here (task #24): Passthrough trims to AVI run their sound late. A stream copy keeps packets from where the demuxer's seek lands, before the in-point (here the file's start: the fixture has one keyframe). MKV and MP4 store that pre-roll as a later video start and stay in sync. AVI cannot, so the extra sound plays under the first picture: 33–100 ms late at frames 1–3 after the fix (1.28 s early before it), and 667 ms late at frame 20, with or without it. small_clip.mp4 trimmed from frame 20 to AVI is 0.395 s late. Pre-trimming the NUT copy at the in-point was tried and is worse in MKV: ffmpeg rebases the copy's first packet (29 ms in) to 0, so the sound lands 29 ms late.

Limits

  • One H.264 B-frame fixture (PCM) and one MPEG-4 ASP B-frame variant (MP3), at 29.97 fps, on macOS arm64 with FFmpeg 9.0. MPEG-2 in AVI has the same delay (#173) and takes the same path, but its sound was not measured.
  • Sample matching finds exact positions for PCM. For MP3 it skips the decoder's first 4096 samples and matches within 2e-3, so it reads the position, not the bits.
  • MKV with B-frames (no AVFMT_SEEK_TO_PTS either) loses up to ~95 ms of sound below ~0.13 s without an error. The worker doesn't know an MKV's B-frame delay, so that's outside rule 9.
  • Subtitle streams on input 1 of a delayed AVI trimmed under 0.5 s still see the failed seek (AVI's XSUB cannot be copied to MKV anyway).
