Task #58: what the output conformance matrix found, fixed

2026-11-17 · macOS arm64, deps 1.16.0 (bundled FFmpeg 9.0.2) · task #58, filed by task #49 (its report)

Five findings, five causes, all fixed. Two were worker bugs (a dropped frame served as another picture; a primed AVI trim cut short), one a decoder choice in the Audio Restoration render, one the reset join, and one the matrix's own oracle. knownFailures is empty. Each fix was checked against the pre-fix worker, built from dc9e36c: every case it fixes fails there.

4. pipe_source served a frame it had dropped as another picture

The source is read from a pipe, forward only, and pipe_source kept the 40 frames behind the last one read. A frame asked for after it left that window came back as whatever frame had been read last, silently. The matrix saw it as pictures 3–7 of an output holding source picture 99.

Reproduction

Nothing but the pipe: conf_plain.mp4 decoded to raw frames, piped to vspipe running a script that is only create_pipe_clip(…, cache_frames=40), each output compared by framemd5 with a direct decode, 16 runs at once (pipe_source_stress.sh):

readerrunswrong runsruns that raised
before (dc9e36c), first sample3206–
before (dc9e36c), second sample3208–
after, first sample32000
after, second sample32000

In the failing runs the wrong frames were output 1–7, 4–7, 5–7 or 6–7, each holding whatever the reader had read last (source 53, 69, 74, 99), not always 99. An instrumented reader (not kept) showed the cause: in both failing runs out of 160, frames 6 and 7 reached the callback after the reader had read to frame 100 (92–93 frames behind it); passing runs never fell more than 38 behind. vspipe requests a new frame each time any frame completes, so one early frame whose callback is late lets the whole clip go past it. Sleeping inside the callback for frame 3 did not reproduce it (the callbacks run one at a time, so a sleep there stalls everything): the late frame is one whose callback hasn't started.

Fix

The reader keeps every frame until it has been served and is cache_frames behind the oldest frame not yet served (not merely behind the last frame read), with a cap for frames the script never asks for (a decimation, a preview's single frame): 512 MB beyond the window, at least 16 frames. A frame asked for after it was dropped raises a VapourSynth error naming it, so a pass whose radius is under-declared now fails the job instead of writing wrong frames. app/test/pipe_source_order_test.dart (push gate) drives the reader in the measured order with a fake pipe, and checks the window, the cap and the end of stream.

Limits: the failure rate depends on load, so the counts are one machine's on one afternoon; the deterministic test is the guard.

1. Restore + Convert AAC into AVI, trimmed: one picture short

Task #38 reads every sound input of a Convert into AVI late by the encoder's priming P (AAC: 1024 samples), so that the priming the encoder puts in front lands where the sound was. The restored track is exactly the window long, so read from P it ends P before the picture on ffmpeg's timeline, and -shortest dropped the last picture. The same arguments with plain ffmpeg (shortest_priming.sh, 50 pictures, a 2.000 s WAV):

-ss P on the exact WAV, -shortest (before)    pictures 49, audio 2.005333 s
no priming seek, -shortest                    pictures 50, audio 2.026667 s
WAV P longer, -shortest                       pictures 50, audio 2.026667 s
no -shortest                                  pictures 50, audio 2.005333 s
-t 2 instead of -shortest (after)             pictures 50, audio 2.005333 s

Not only Restore. A Convert into AVI trimmed to the source's last picture reads a sound that also ends with the window. The matrix had no such trim; four new cases (avi/convert-{aac,libmp3lame,ac3}/plain/tail, avi/restore-convert-aac/plain/tail, frames 50..99) all store 49 pictures on the pre-fix worker.

Fix: under a priming, a trimmed encode ends with -t the window's length in place of -shortest (pipeline_executor::encode_end_args, both builders). Every other job keeps its arguments.

2. Audio Restoration of a seeked MP2: one 16-bit step off

FFmpeg's default decoder for MPEG layers I and II is the fixed-point one (mp2, s16p output), listed ahead of mp2float; layer III defaults to mp3float. The fixed-point synthesis rounds with an error it carries from frame to frame, so a decode that starts at a seek rounds differently from one that starts at 0. No filter is involved (mp2_seek_decoder.sh, conf_late_video.ts from 1.090 s, the render's chain, against a decode of the whole stream):

default_near         vs full_fixed.f32   offset 52321: 41377 of 96000 samples differ, max 1.000 LSB16
default_near_noaf    vs full_fixed.f32   offset 52321: 41377 of 96000 samples differ, max 1.000 LSB16
default_near         vs full_float.f32   offset 52321: 95996 of 96000 samples differ, max 1.047 LSB16
float_near           vs full_float.f32   offset 52321: 0 of 96000 samples differ, max 0.000 LSB16
float_mid            vs full_float.f32   offset 125281: 0 of 96000 samples differ, max 0.000 LSB16

Correction to task #49's report. It said Convert to FLAC/PCM of the same seeked MP2 was exact. It differs in the same samples; it passed because the oracle allows one step at the output's depth, and Convert writes 16-bit. The restored track is 24-bit, so the same step failed it.

Fix: the render reads MP1/MP2 with mp1float/mp2float (audio_restoration::render_decoder), which is also the more precise decode for an f32 render. Convert and the analysis keep FFmpeg's default. The oracle compares a restore against the render's decoder (MatrixCase.referenceDecoder).

3. Delayed AVI copied into AVI from frame 2: the oracle, not the copy

Where each source's first click sits after its flash, on its own timeline (click_lags.sh):

conf_plain.mp4       click 0.2001 s, flash 0.2000 s: the click +0.1 ms after it
conf_late_video.ts   click 2.6101 s, flash 2.6100 s: the click +0.1 ms after it
conf_delayed.avi     click 0.2231 s, flash 0.2000 s: the click +23.1 ms after it
conf_reset.mpg       click 0.7401 s, flash 0.7400 s: the click +0.1 ms after it

The delayed AVI's MP3 was written by libmp3lame into AVI, which cannot mark the encoder's start-up (1,105 samples), so every player plays it 23 ms late: real AVI captures with MP3 look like this. Its packets are 24 ms; the in-point 0.080 s falls in the packet at 0.072, which the copy keeps (task #24's nearest-packet cut), 8 ms early, so the content plays 8 ms later: 23 + 8 = 31 ms, as measured. The mid trim's in-point (1.600) keeps the packet at 1.608: 23 − 8 = 15 ms, which passed. The worker keeps the source's timing within half a packet, as designed.

Fix (test only): the matrix measures each source's own lag per flash and expects each output click there, within one packet as before.

5. A reset whose sound ends off the picture grid joined 16 ms late

conf_reset_offgrid.mpg resets at picture 50 (2.0 s = 83.3 MP2 frames), so its first part's sound runs 16 ms past its last picture, as a DVD cell's may. ffmpeg's discontinuity correction (the joined copy, without -copyts) takes one offset per segment from whichever stream shows the jump first, using that stream's next timestamp: here the sound's. The worker now makes a JoinPlan from the scan (each packet's DTS, duration and byte position): each segment moves so its first picture follows the last by one frame, every stream alike, applied by setts per stream under -copyts, with a sound packet overhanging the next segment by more than half of itself dropped (reset_join.sh):

ffmpeg conf_reset              rate 25/1    pictures 100, picture steps [0.04], picture 50 at 2.0000 s; sound frames 167, irregular sound steps []
worker conf_reset              rate 25/1    pictures 100, picture steps [0.04], picture 50 at 2.0000 s; sound frames 167, irregular sound steps []
ffmpeg conf_reset_offgrid      rate 125/1   pictures 100, picture steps [0.04, 0.056], picture 50 at 2.0160 s; sound frames 168, irregular sound steps []
worker conf_reset_offgrid      rate 25/1    pictures 100, picture steps [0.04], picture 50 at 2.0000 s; sound frames 167, irregular sound steps [(2.006, 0.032)]

On the grid the two copies time every picture and sound frame alike. Off it, the picture now stays on the grid, and the sound has one 8 ms gap where its overhanging packet went (the job log says so). The sound after the join is within half a packet either way, which a copy can't improve on.

Two traps on the way, both in the plan: the MPEG audio frames the parser splits out of one PES carry no byte position, so a stream that resets is cut by its packet index (N), and only streams without a reset of their own (subtitles) by position (POS); and a packet with no DTS must keep none (if(eq(DTS,NOPTS),NOPTS,…)), or the copy's DTS stop increasing. Without a plan (no durations, streams resetting a different number of times) the copy is exactly as before. The off-grid matrix test is an ordinary test now; integration_seek_alignment_test.dart's seek_reset_* fixtures and the DVD import test pass through the new copy.

Limits: two synthetic program streams; no DVD with angles or a broadcast TS with a reset was available. Task #55 (reset scan) changes the same scan and should rebase on this.
